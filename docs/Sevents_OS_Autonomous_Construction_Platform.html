The Industry Problem #1: Software Fragmentation

Why Construction Projects Bleed Margins Without a Single Unified ERP

Disconnected spreadsheets, WhatsApp coordination, and siloed software cost contractors 15–20% of project margin.

❌ Spreadsheet Chaos

Critical BOQs, rate analyses, and stock registers live in 40+ disconnected Excel sheets. One broken formula leads to six-figure bidding errors.

❌ Site vs. Head Office Disconnect

Site diaries written on paper or WhatsApp. Head office commercial managers only discover project delays 3 to 4 weeks after they happen on site.

❌ Inventory Blindspots

Stores run out of essential rebar or cement mid-pour because procurement and site stores don't share a real-time stock balance ledger.

❌ Unrecovered Variation Claims

Contractors lose legitimate Extension of Time (EOT) and cost claims because daily site disruption events aren't backed by contractual evidence.
