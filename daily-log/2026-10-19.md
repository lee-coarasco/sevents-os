### In plain words (for the owner)
- **What got done**: M23 delivers full contractual governance, commercial variation tracking, FIDIC claim defense, and insurance/bond management for construction projects. Legal and commercial teams can manage prime and subcontracts, handle site instructions and variation orders with automatic budget adjustments, protect time-bar deadlines under standard FIDIC 28-day notice rules, and track high-value performance bonds and bank guarantees.
- **What you can test now**:
  1. Open the sidebar and click **Contracts & Claims** (`/contracts`).
  2. On the **Contract Repository** tab, view your contracts or click **+ Register Contract** to record an EPC contract or subcontract.
  3. Switch to **Variation Orders**, click **+ Request Variation** to propose a scope or design change with cost and delay estimates. Click **Approve / Certify** to approve it, and observe how the parent contract's revised value and completion date automatically update.
  4. Switch to **Claims & EOT**, click **+ Submit Claim / EOT** to log a delay event. Notice how the system automatically computes the 28-day notice bar and highlights late notices with a time-bar warning badge. Authorized users can click **Assess / Certify** to certify approved EOT days.
  5. Switch to **Bonds & Insurance** to register Performance Bonds, APGs, or CAR Insurance policies and manage their active/released states.
